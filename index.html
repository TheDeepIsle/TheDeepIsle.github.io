Mumble Setup Guide

🎙️

Mumble is Required on The Deep Isle

We use Mumble for positional proximity voice chat — players closer to you sound louder, audio comes from left/right based on position. Mumble is required to play on this server. If the server does not detect you on Mumble within 15 minutes of joining, you will be kicked automatically.

Download Mumble Download Deep Isle Plugin
01
Install Mumble & the Deep Isle Plugin
▼
  • Download Mumble from mumble.info/downloads and install it like any normal program.
  • Download the TheDeepIsleExileVoicePluginSetup.exe Spatial Audio Plugin using the button above.
  • Run the plugin installer — it installs automatically to the correct Mumble folder.
What the plugin does: Players closer to you = louder. Players further away = quieter. Audio comes from left/right depending on their position relative to yours. You need this plugin to hear people correctly in game.
02
Add the Server
▼
  • Open Mumble and click Server → Connect → Add New
  • Enter the server details exactly as shown below and click OK to connect.

03
Enable the Spatial Audio Plugin
▼
  • In Mumble click Configure → Settings → Plugins
  • Find "Exile Voice - Spatial Audio" in the list
  • Make sure the checkbox next to it is ticked/enabled
  • Close Mumble completely and restart it
After restarting you should see a message in the Mumble log confirming the plugin loaded: "Exile Voice - Spatial Audio: [exile_voice] loaded"
04
Verify In-Game
▼
  • Launch The Isle and join The Deep Isle server
  • Make sure Mumble is open and you are connected to the Mumble server
  • Mumble and the game server will communicate and verify your position automatically
If it's working: You will receive no messages from the server about Mumble.
If it's NOT working: You will begin receiving automated messages advising that Mumble is required. Fix the issue within 15 minutes or you will be kicked automatically.
05
Set Up Push-To-Talk (Required)
▼
Push-To-Talk is required on this server at all times. Hot mics are not permitted.
  • In Mumble click Configure → Settings → Shortcuts
  • Click Add
  • In the Function dropdown select Push-to-Talk
  • Click the Shortcut field and press the key or mouse button you want to use
  • Click OK to save
Recommended: Mouse button 5 (M5) keeps your hands on keyboard/mouse without needing to reach for a key.
06
Disable Notification Sounds (Strongly Recommended)
▼

Mumble has a lot of notification sounds by default that will interrupt your gameplay. It is strongly recommended to turn all of these off.

  • Click Configure → Settings → Messages
  • Untick ALL checkboxes in the Notification column — this removes all notification sounds

Also remove mic activation noise:

  • Go to Configure → Settings → Audio Input → Misc
  • Make sure Push To Talk and Voice Activity audio cue checkboxes are both unchecked
07
Audio Quality Fixes (Very Important)
▼
These settings significantly improve audio quality. Please complete all steps.
  • Go to Configure → Settings → Audio Input → Compression
  • Uncheck "Allow low-delay mode"
  • Turn "Audio per packet" slider to maximum (60ms)
  • Turn "Quality" slider to maximum (192.0 kb/s)
✅ And you're done! Enjoy the server!

Law of the Land

Server Rules

01

Age Limit 18+ Only

This server is 18+ only. If you are verified to be underage you will be kicked and banned. There are no exceptions.

02

⚔️ In-Game Combat Rules

  • No Teamkilling: You cannot intentionally kill a member of your own group. If issues arise, disengage instead of escalating.
  • Group Leaving: Do not leave a group and immediately start attacking. Give players time to relocate.
  • RCON Ceasefire: All fights must stop immediately once the RCON restart message appears. Do not kill players who are safelogging for restart.
  • Disconnect / Crash Abuse: Killing players who have crashed or are disconnecting is against the spirit of fair play.

Admin Enforcement If admins catch you excessively killing disconnected players, your Dino will be slayed — no exceptions.

No Tickets We do NOT accept reports for disconnect deaths as we cannot verify them.

03

🦖 No Mixpacking / Megaherding

  • Mixpacking: Different dinosaur species working or fighting together is NOT ALLOWED.
  • Megaherding: Excessively large groups (see limits below) fighting together is NOT ALLOWED.
  • Allowed: Being near other species without protecting or benefiting from them.
  • Not Allowed: Defending, assisting, or fighting alongside another species.
Automation Warning: If you are within range of other species or oversized groups for 15+ minutes, you will receive an automated message. Shortly after, you will take health damage until you separate.

04

🚫 No Third Partying

Do NOT join fights that are already in progress. Wait until the fight is clearly over before engaging.

  • Jumping into an active fight or attacking already engaged players
  • Grouping mid-fight to help someone

EXCEPTIONS:

  • 🐊 Deinosuchus: Exempt due to its ambush playstyle. However, Deinosuchus may NOT third-party other Deinosuchus in combat.
  • 🦎 Herrerasaurus: Exempt due to its ambush playstyle.
05

🎙️ Voice Chat & Interactions

  • Push-To-Talk (PTT) Required at all times to prevent hot mics and maintain immersion.
  • Respect: Abuse, harassment, or inappropriate behavior in voice channels will result in immediate moderation action or bans.
  • No Music in VC. Soundboards are allowed if used responsibly — disruptive use will not be tolerated.
06

📊 Group & Nesting Limits

  • Mutation Rule: Once you hit your first mutation, you count towards the group number. If the group is full, you must leave.
  • Nesting-Slay Mechanic: To prevent over-nesting, hatchlings will be automatically slain if the species is already at or past its cap.
📜

Have Fun, Be Courteous & Respectful

We are all here to have fun and enjoy the game. Be courteous, respectful, and kind. Treat everyone else how you would want to be treated. Also... eat some dinosaurs!
